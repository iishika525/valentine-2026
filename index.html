The Journey We Share... ❤️

Back in class 7th ✨

Remember that day I had no pencil? You quietly broke yours in two and handed me one half. That small gesture felt like the world.

Shared Smiles 😊

We used to place our bags in front of us, hiding our hands, just so we could hold them without anyone noticing.

Our Bond ❤️

That moment when your cheeky attempt missed and landed on my ear… and you ran off, leaving me smiling forever.

Always Together 🤝

After all those years, I saw you again. Just a quick look, and my eyes welled up… it felt like seeing something I’d been waiting for forever.

Pure Happiness 🌟

and then… we met. We met again, Yash. Sitting next to you after all these years, being able to touch you, to feel you… it was one of the greatest days of my life. Even writing this makes me so emotional. Damn, I love you so much.

Thinking of You 💭

Remember the first time you hugged me? I don’t know what you felt, but I felt something so pure, so divine. I’ll never forget it. I felt so protective of you, like you were my world.

A Precious Moment 📸

Then we went to Manali… something I thought was impossible actually happened, and it happened with you. I was so, so happy.

GoodByes.. ❤️

But we can’t always be happy, can we? The distance was calling—you were leaving for Canada. We were stepping into a long-distance relationship. It was a big decision, but we were strong… stronger than ever at that moment.

Still Close 💛

In this phase, we were happy. Every time you saw something beautiful, you’d video call me. Even with the distance, we never felt apart. We moved freely in our own worlds.

Silent Distance 🌙

Like every other relationship, we had our fights. But this one felt different. I wasn’t just hurt—I felt neglected. Even though distance had never made me feel far from you, I started feeling it when you began socializing more. It felt like you were slowly leaving me behind.

Quiet Struggle 🌧️

I needed closeness, you needed space. Neither of us was wrong.

Staying Strong 💪

That day, nothing else mattered. Knowing your mom wasn’t well filled us all with concern.

Family First 🌱

Without a second thought, you booked your tickets and came home. That’s who you are—loving, responsible, and deeply devoted to your family. I was so proud of you.

Answered Prayers 🙏

By God’s grace, your mom was healthy—stronger than ever. And I got to see you too… maybe it was destiny.

Our Common Dreams ☁️

We aren't just waiting; we are building. Building a future where we never have to say goodbye at an airport again.

Lessons Learned 🌱

History repeated itself. This time, I was the one enjoying life and giving you less priority—maybe out of hurt, maybe as revenge. It wasn’t right, and I’m still sorry.

Unmet Needs 🤍

Life was heavy for me then, and still is in many ways. I hoped you’d have time for me, and I know you were exhausted with long shifts. You weren’t wrong—but what I needed wasn’t more time, just a little emotional support.

Inner Chaos 🌫️

So many things came together—my future, my career, university pressure, and my emotions. I felt overwhelmed and scared of losing what mattered to me. I just wanted to heal and make things right.

Safe Anchor 🤍

I know, Yash, you’ve always been with me—you’d never think bad about me or hurt me. My real battle was with my own mind, and I’ll always be grateful you said, ‘Main tere saath hoon.’

Chosen Patience ⭐

The real culprit was my mind. It daydreams, overthinks, and runs at a speed even I can’t stop. I know I need help, I’m working on it—and I’m truly sorry.

You Are Home 🏡

I know you don’t like it when I overthink or say things out of fear, but this is something I am still learning to handle. I am taking my medication properly now and trying every day to be better. In this difficult phase, all I have wanted is you by my side, consistently and truly. I don’t want anyone else stepping in or taking advantage of my vulnerability. I trust only you, and I want to keep trusting you. I have never thought of anyone else, because you are irreplaceable to me. You are my family, and family cannot be replaced. We fight sometimes, but who doesn’t? So… be my Valentine 🫂I can't wait to hold your hand again.
